Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026. MaPC is made by Steale LLC.

Short version: MaPC has no telemetry, and this website has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

The MaPC app

  • It collects no personal or usage data.
  • You sign in to Steam, Epic Games and GOG on their own pages. Their login tokens are kept on your Mac, in a folder only your user account can read. MaPC never sees your passwords.
  • It connects to the internet to download game engines and tools (from our release page), cover art and store data, and, if you support MaPC, to check your license with Gumroad.
  • If you use save backup, saves are copied only to the folder you choose (for example iCloud Drive). They are never sent to us.
  • The Wall of Fame list is downloaded from this website. It holds only names supporters chose to publish.

Supporting MaPC

Payments are handled by Gumroad, which is the seller of record. Gumroad receives your payment details, email and anything you type at checkout, under its own privacy policy. We receive the sale details Gumroad shows us, such as your email, the amount and the name you chose for the Wall of Fame. We use them to send your license, give support and keep the wall up to date.

When you activate a license, and about once a week afterwards, MaPC sends your license key to Gumroad to confirm that it’s valid and hasn’t been refunded. Your license is stored in your Mac’s Keychain.

The Wall of Fame

A name is published only if you type one in the Wall of Fame field at checkout. It appears on this website and in the app. To remove it, email support@mapc.games.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its domain is managed with Cloudflare. They may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, under their own policies. We don’t add analytics, advertising or cookies. Downloads are served from GitHub Releases.

Email

Mail sent to support@mapc.games is forwarded to our inbox. We use it only to answer you.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@mapc.games.
