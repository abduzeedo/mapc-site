Terms & refunds

Last updated October 11, 2026.

What you’re buying

MaPC is free to download and use. Supporting MaPC is an optional, one-time purchase that gives you a license key. The key unlocks the “Pro” extras in MaPC 1.x: save backup, playtime stats, keep Mac awake, game shortcuts, the menu bar launcher, Siri and Shortcuts, Retina resolution, the performance HUD and ⌘ as Control, and a place on the Wall of Fame if you choose to add a name. We may add more extras to 1.x over time. A future major version may be sold separately.

Who sells it

MaPC is made by Steale LLC. Payments are processed by Gumroad, which is the merchant of record. It collects any sales tax or VAT and sends you the receipt. Gumroad’s terms apply to the payment.

Price and tiers

You choose the amount, from $4.99. The tier names (Insert Coin, High Scorer, Arcade Hero and Legend) set your row on the Wall of Fame. Every tier unlocks the same extras.

Using your license

  • Your license is personal and works on up to 5 Macs that you own or control. To move it, email support@mapc.games.
  • MaPC checks your license when you activate it and about once a week. It keeps working for 30 days if it can’t reach the server.
  • Don’t share or resell your key. Keys that are shared widely may be turned off.
  • Use of MaPC is also governed by the license agreement.

Refunds

You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying, no questions asked. Reply to your receipt email or write to support@mapc.games and we’ll refund you through Gumroad. When a purchase is refunded, its license key stops working, and MaPC returns to the free version the next time it checks your license. The free app and your games are never affected. Your name is removed from the Wall of Fame the next time we update it.

If you’re in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with consumer rights that can’t be waived, those rights still apply.

No guarantee for any game

MaPC runs Windows games on Macs through compatibility software. We don’t promise that any particular game will run, or run well. Please try the free app first. Games with kernel-level anti-cheat can’t run on macOS.
