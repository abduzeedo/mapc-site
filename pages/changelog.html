What’s new

MaPC 1.0.6 Beta LATEST

October 2026

  • Epic Games: fixed “The epic environment hasn’t been set up yet” when pressing Play. MaPC now sets up the Epic Windows environment on the first Play, and again after you reset it in Settings.

MaPC 1.0.5 Beta

October 2026

  • PlayStation-style controllers now work in games that only support Xbox controllers: DualShock 4, DualSense and the Backbone One PlayStation Edition. A new Controllers tab in Settings shows what your Mac sees, with a switch (on by default).
  • MaPC can now update itself: Check for Updates in the MaPC menu, plus optional automatic updates in Settings. Updates are signed and checked before they install, and a running game is never interrupted. Versions before 1.0.5 need this one manual download.

MaPC 1.0.4 Beta

October 2026

  • Clearer support screen: everything that runs your games stays free; supporting MaPC unlocks the Pro extras and helps cover the AI token costs of building the beta.

MaPC 1.0.3 Beta

October 2026

  • The ripple when you press Play now spreads across the whole artwork, on any window size.

MaPC 1.0.2 Beta

October 2026

  • Updated license agreement: MaPC is pre-release software, and experimental DirectX 12 compatibility is explained (free, not part of MaPC Pro, and only with Apple’s Game Porting Toolkit if you install it yourself).
  • Clearer notes for DirectX 12 games and during DirectX 12 setup.

MaPC 1.0.1 Beta

October 2026

  • Experimental DirectX 12 compatibility for advanced users who install Apple’s Game Porting Toolkit themselves (free, not part of MaPC Pro). See details.
  • Games that use the Rockstar Games Launcher: the launcher now starts and its sign-in window shows up (tested with Red Dead Redemption 2).
  • The DirectX 12 setup window was unreadable over some game artwork. Fixed.
  • Settings › About shows the right version.
  • Engines now include the full license texts of everything inside them.

MaPC 1.0.0 Beta LAUNCH

October 2026

  • Install and play Windows games from Steam, Epic Games and GOG.
  • DirectX 11 through DXVK.
  • Per-game graphics settings, play time, logs and known-issue notes.
  • Supporter extras: save backup and restore, playtime stats, keep Mac awake, game shortcuts, menu bar launcher, Siri and Shortcuts, Retina, performance HUD, ⌘ as Control, and the Wall of Fame.

Every release, with its SHA-256 and the source of the engines we build, is on the releases page.
