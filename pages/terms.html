Refunds

You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying, no questions asked. Reply to your receipt email or write to {{EMAIL}} and we’ll refund you through Gumroad. When a purchase is refunded, its license key stops working, and MaPC returns to the free version the next time it checks your license. The free app and your games are never affected. Your name is removed from the Wall of Fame within 30 days.

Save backup and restore

Save backup copies the files you choose to a folder you choose, and restore copies them back, replacing files with the same name. We try to keep a safety copy before a restore, but backups are a convenience, not a guarantee. Check that your backups work, and keep another copy of saves you can’t afford to lose.
