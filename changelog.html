What’s new

MaPC 1.1.6 Beta LATEST

October 2026

  • Fixed: if you installed the DirectX 12 engine 2.0.1, games kept crashing at start on macOS 26 even after updating MaPC. MaPC now notices engines that have been replaced and offers to update them (2.0.1 to 2.0.2, and 1.0.0 to 1.0.1). It asks before downloading anything, moves your Windows environments to the new engine, and keeps the old one until the new one works.
  • Check Last Launch now says when a game stopped because its engine has been replaced, with a button to update it.

MaPC 1.1.5 Beta

October 2026

  • Fixed: after a game switched Steam from the DirectX 12 engine (D3DMetal) back to DXVK, Steam could fail to start with a “dxgi.dll” error. MaPC now puts the right graphics files back, and repairs bottles where they went missing.
  • Fixed: Steam no longer asks you to sign in again every time a game switches engines. You may need to sign in to Steam (or the Rockstar Games Launcher) one more time after updating if you last signed in on the DirectX 12 engine; after that, sign-ins carry over.

MaPC 1.1.4 Beta

October 2026

  • Fixed: on macOS 26, the DirectX 12 engine closed as soon as it started. The new engine 2.0.2 fixes it. If you use DirectX 12 games, open Settings › Engine and install it.
  • New: 32-bit DirectX 10 and 11 games (Cuphead, for example) run with DXMT in the extended engine. MaPC tells you on the game’s page when a game needs it, with a download button, and picks it automatically.
  • Fixed: WineD3D no longer loads DXVK by mistake, and graphics files from one game no longer affect the next (the Rockstar Launcher could crash).
  • New: MaPC Help (Help menu, ⌘?) with getting started, what MaPC needs, which games work, and common problems.
  • New: Check Last Launch on a game’s page reads its last log and names known problems, with a button for the fix. On macOS 26 and later, Apple Intelligence can explain them in plain words, on your Mac.
  • Steam: when a game needs different graphics than Steam started with, MaPC offers to restart Steam (or does it without asking, if you choose).
  • Steam’s startup card appears as soon as you press Play, lists each step, and is easier to read.
  • Game videos: Settings › Engine shows whether GStreamer is installed and where to get it. MaPC doesn’t include it.
  • Updated license agreement and privacy page (Apple Intelligence explanations, GStreamer). You’ll be asked to accept the agreement again.
  • New engine 1.0.1: the same as 1.0.0 for games, smaller (an unused web engine was removed), and with complete license notices.

MaPC 1.1.3 Beta

October 18, 2026

  • Fixed: opening Steam before a game could leave DirectX 12 games without the D3DMetal graphics setup they need. Steam now starts with a graphics backend provided by its engine.
  • Tested opening Steam first and then launching Red Dead Redemption 2 through the running client, reaching a responsive game menu.

MaPC 1.1.2 Beta

October 2026

  • More reliable first-time Steam setup: incomplete installations download and verify the full client.
  • New Repair Steam action in Settings › Stores checks client files while keeping installed games and saves.
  • Steam interface repairs keep working after its startup card is hidden, through updates and helper restarts.
  • Startup status can recover after failed attempts and follows replacement log files correctly.
  • Engine changes, environment resets and app updates check the actual running Windows environments.
  • Save restore stops if the safety backup cannot be created, protecting current progress.
  • Steam launch arguments are passed through to games; external Steam libraries are monitored for changes.
  • Problem reports keep the previous launch attempts and include engine and process exit details.

MaPC 1.1.0 Beta

October 2026

  • While Steam starts, MaPC shows what it’s doing: checking for updates, downloading one, loading its window or waiting for you to sign in. Steam’s own window is often black during those steps. If it takes too long, you can restart Steam or send us its logs.
  • Fixed: after a Steam client update, Steam’s window could stay black until you restarted it. MaPC now fixes it while Steam starts when you open it from MaPC.
  • Open Steam brings Steam to the front when it’s already running, instead of starting it again.
  • New: Help › Report a Problem collects your logs into a file you can email us. Your name, your Mac’s name, your Steam account and email addresses are removed first.
  • New: Help › Activity shows what’s running in each store’s Windows environment, and lets you stop it.
  • Steam has a Refresh button, and finds games in Steam libraries on your Mac’s drive or an external drive.
  • Fixed: signing out of Epic or GOG, or uninstalling a Steam game, erased its playtime and settings.
  • Fixed: “Sign in with Google/Apple” buttons on the Epic and GOG sign-in pages did nothing, and Epic’s sign-in page could stay blank.
  • Fixed: loading your Epic library could hang.

MaPC 1.0.9 Beta

October 2026

  • MaPC’s game code is now open source: the part that runs games is published under the Apache License 2.0 at github.com/abduzeedo/mapc-engine. Settings › About › Acknowledgements links to it.
  • Fixed: the “Source Code for Engine Components” link in Acknowledgements now opens the engine releases where that source is published.

MaPC 1.0.8 Beta

October 2026

  • Settings › Engines now describes the DirectX 12 engine as “Wine 11.0, from CrossOver 26.3.0 source”: it’s Wine we build from the source CodeWeavers publishes, not CrossOver itself.

MaPC 1.0.7 Beta

October 2026

  • The Play icon next to search is now a button that says “Open Steam” (and “Open Ubisoft Connect” or “Open EA app” in those sections).
  • Playtime graphs are black on light and white on dark, so they read clearly on any page color.
  • Sidebar: icons now match the text color in the light theme, and they line up with the section headers.

MaPC 1.0.6 Beta

October 2026

  • Epic Games: fixed “The epic environment hasn’t been set up yet” when pressing Play. MaPC now sets up the Epic Windows environment on the first Play, and again after you reset it in Settings.

MaPC 1.0.5 Beta

October 2026

  • PlayStation-style controllers now work in games that only support Xbox controllers: DualShock 4, DualSense and the Backbone One PlayStation Edition. A new Controllers tab in Settings shows what your Mac sees, with a switch (on by default).
  • MaPC can now update itself: Check for Updates in the MaPC menu, plus optional automatic updates in Settings. Updates are signed and checked before they install, and a running game is never interrupted. Versions before 1.0.5 need this one manual download.

MaPC 1.0.4 Beta

October 2026

  • Clearer support screen: everything that runs your games stays free; supporting MaPC unlocks the Pro extras and helps cover the AI token costs of building the beta.

MaPC 1.0.3 Beta

October 2026

  • The ripple when you press Play now spreads across the whole artwork, on any window size.

MaPC 1.0.2 Beta

October 2026

  • Updated license agreement: MaPC is pre-release software, and experimental DirectX 12 compatibility is explained (free, not part of MaPC Pro, and only with Apple’s Game Porting Toolkit if you install it yourself).
  • Clearer notes for DirectX 12 games and during DirectX 12 setup.

MaPC 1.0.1 Beta

October 2026

  • Experimental DirectX 12 compatibility for advanced users who install Apple’s Game Porting Toolkit themselves (free, not part of MaPC Pro). See details.
  • Games that use the Rockstar Games Launcher: the launcher now starts and its sign-in window shows up (tested with Red Dead Redemption 2).
  • The DirectX 12 setup window was unreadable over some game artwork. Fixed.
  • Settings › About shows the right version.
  • Engines now include the full license texts of everything inside them.

MaPC 1.0.0 Beta LAUNCH
